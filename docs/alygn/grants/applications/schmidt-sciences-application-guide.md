## Section 1: About Schmidt Sciences

### Overview

Schmidt Sciences is a philanthropic organization founded in 2024 by Eric Schmidt (former Google CEO) and Wendy Schmidt. It funds unconventional science and technology research with a focus on talent, technology, and global challenges.

The organization emerged from the Schmidt Family Foundation and represents a significant new player in the AI safety funding landscape, with deep technical expertise and a willingness to support high-risk, unconventional research approaches.

### Research Agenda (Three Aims)

Schmidt Sciences has structured their research agenda around three interconnected aims:

**Aim 1: Characterize and forecast misalignment in frontier AI systems**
- Understanding how and why AI systems become misaligned
- Developing predictive models for misalignment risks
- Early warning systems and indicators

**Aim 2: Develop generalizable measurements and interventions** ⭐ *Primary ALYGN fit*
- Measurement methodologies that work across different models and contexts
- Interventions that can be applied broadly
- Validated benchmarks with rigorous validity arguments

**Aim 3: Oversee AI systems with superhuman capabilities and address multi-agent risks**
- Oversight mechanisms for systems beyond human comprehension
- Multi-agent interaction safety
- Scalable monitoring and control systems

### Selection Criteria

Applications are evaluated on:

1. **Research Agenda Fit** — Alignment with the three aims above
2. **Scientific Quality and Rigor** — Methodological soundness, validity arguments
3. **Potential Impact** — Significance of contribution to trustworthy AI
4. **Feasibility and Scope** — Realistic timeline and deliverables
5. **Team Expertise** — Relevant experience and capabilities
6. **Cost Effectiveness** — Value for money, efficient resource use

### Common Non-Competitive Reasons

**Critical:** Avoid these pitfalls that frequently disqualify applications:

- ❌ **Lack of core focus** — Trying to do too many things; unclear central contribution
- ❌ **Tools/benchmarks without validity argument** — Creating measurements without showing they measure what they claim
- ❌ **Vague methods** — "We will explore..." instead of "We will implement..."
- ❌ **Unclear success/failure outcomes** — No clear criteria for what constitutes success

#### 3. Research Proposal (Main Content)

This is the core of the application. Suggested structure:

---

**Problem Statement**

Current AI safety mechanisms rely predominantly on software-based controls that can be bypassed, modified, or circumvented by sophisticated actors. There exists no robust technical infrastructure for enforcing safety compliance at the hardware level with transparent, auditable guarantees that remain valid under optimization pressure.

Furthermore, existing evaluation methods lack rigorous validity arguments — they cannot demonstrate:
- **Construct validity:** That measurements actually capture the safety properties they claim to measure
- **Predictive validity:** That measurements predict real-world safety outcomes
- **Robustness under optimization:** That measurements remain valid when systems are optimized to perform well on them

This gap is particularly critical as AI systems approach and exceed human capabilities in domains where safety failures could have severe consequences.

---

**Research Objectives**

1. **Develop hardware-attested evaluation methodologies** for AI safety compliance that provide tamper-resistant evidence of system states
2. **Design cryptoeconomic mechanisms** that align operator behavior with safety goals through incentive-compatible mechanisms
3. **Create automated verification systems** with blockchain-based audit trails enabling transparent, retrospective analysis
4. **Establish validity arguments** for hardware-based safety measurements demonstrating construct, predictive, and optimization robustness validity

---

**Technical Approach**

*Hardware Attestation Layer:*
- Leverage Trusted Execution Environments (Intel SGX, AMD SEV, or ARM TrustZone) for tamper-resistant execution
- Develop attestation protocols that cryptographically prove compliance status without revealing sensitive model weights
- Create hardware-enforced logging mechanisms that cannot be bypassed through software manipulation

*Cryptoeconomic Mechanism Design:*
- Design Bitcoin-based incentive structures that reward compliance verification and penalize violations
- Develop staking mechanisms where operators bond capital against safety compliance
- Create automated slashing conditions triggered by attestation failures

*Monitoring and Verification:*
- Implement real-time monitoring of safety conditions via hardware attestation
- Develop blockchain-based audit trails providing immutable records of system states
- Create automated verification protocols that can operate without human intervention

*Benchmark Development:*
- Construct benchmarks with formal validity characteristics
- Demonstrate construct validity through theoretical analysis
- Establish predictive validity via correlation with real-world safety outcomes
- Test robustness under adversarial optimization

---

**Alignment with Research Agenda**

*Aim 2: Generalizable Measurements and Interventions*
- Hardware attestation provides **generalizable measurements** — works across different models, architectures, and deployment contexts
- Cryptoeconomic design provides **interventions** — mechanism-based approaches to aligning incentives
- Validity arguments ensure measurements are scientifically rigorous

*Aim 3: Oversight of Superhuman Systems*
- Blockchain audit trails extend **oversight capabilities** to retrospective analysis
- Automated verification addresses **superhuman oversight regimes** where human monitoring is insufficient
- Hardware enforcement provides guarantees that persist even as capabilities advance

---

**Expected Outcomes**

1. **Technical Infrastructure:** Open-source hardware-attested safety compliance system
2. **Validated Benchmarks:** Benchmark suite with documented validity characteristics
3. **Academic Publications:** 2-3 peer-reviewed papers on cryptoeconomic safety mechanisms
4. **Evaluation Framework:** Reusable framework for hardware-based safety verification
5. **Validity Arguments:** Formal analysis of measurement validity for hardware attestation approaches

---

**Competitive Differentiation**

Unlike existing approaches that rely on software controls or human oversight, this project:
- Provides **hardware-level guarantees** that cannot be bypassed through software manipulation
- Creates **automated verification** that scales to superhuman systems
- Establishes **validity arguments** meeting scientific rigor standards
- Develops **generalizable infrastructure** applicable across deployment contexts

## Section 4: Repositioning Strategy for ALYGN

### Critical Constraint

Schmidt Sciences **explicitly excludes** certain research types. From their FAQ and guidance:

- ❌ **Socio-technical research** (governance, policy, ethics)
- ❌ **Red-teaming** (explicitly called out as not fitting)
- ❌ **Formal verification** (excluded from scope)
- ❌ **Catastrophic risk research** (not their focus)

### Required Repositioning

ALYGN must reframe its work to fit Schmidt's technical focus:

| Instead of... | Use... |
|-------------|--------|
| "AI governance institution" | "Technical evaluation infrastructure developer" |
| "Governance and coordination" | "Automated compliance verification systems" |
| "Kill Switch (emergency shutdown)" | "Certified robustness mechanisms for safety-critical interruption" |
| "Cryptoeconomic incentives" | "Mechanism design for safety validation" |
| "Bitcoin blockchain" | "Immutable audit trail infrastructure" |
| "Policy enforcement" | "Technical constraint enforcement" |
| "Multi-stakeholder coordination" | "Distributed verification protocols" |

### Key Reframing Principles

1. **Emphasize evaluation science, not governance**
   - Frame as developing measurement methodologies
   - Focus on benchmark creation and validation
   - Position as scientific infrastructure, not policy infrastructure

2. **Focus on automated verification of safety properties**
   - Automated = technical, scalable, rigorous
   - Verification = measurement, evidence, proof
   - Avoid "oversight" (sounds like human monitoring)

3. **Position as benchmarking methodology research**
   - Benchmarks are core to Schmidt's Aim 2
   - Validity arguments are explicitly valued
   - Generalizable measurements are the goal

4. **Frame as measurement infrastructure for trustworthy AI**
   - Infrastructure = technical, foundational, enabling
   - Measurement = scientific, rigorous, valid
   - Trustworthy AI = directly aligned with program name

### Language Guide

**Use These Terms:**
- Technical infrastructure
- Evaluation methodology
- Measurement validity
- Automated verification
- Benchmark development
- Mechanism design
- Attestation protocols
- Compliance verification
- Safety constraints
- Tamper-resistant systems

**Avoid These Terms:**
- Governance
- Policy
- Coordination
- Oversight (use "monitoring" or "verification")
- Stakeholders (use "operators" or "parties")
- Ethics
- Regulation
- Compliance (in legal sense — use technical sense instead)

## Appendix: Validity Arguments Framework

Schmidt Sciences emphasizes validity arguments for measurements. Here's a framework for ALYGN's application:

### Construct Validity

*Definition:* Does the measurement actually measure the safety property it claims to measure?

*For Hardware Attestation:*
- Theoretical argument: TEEs provide cryptographic guarantees of execution integrity
- Empirical argument: Demonstrate that attestation failures correlate with actual safety violations
- Face validity: Hardware enforcement is prima facie harder to bypass than software

### Predictive Validity

*Definition:* Does the measurement predict real-world safety outcomes?

*For Hardware Attestation:*
- Longitudinal study: Track systems with/without attestation over time
- Correlation analysis: Show attestation status predicts incident rates
- Simulation: Model how attestation would have prevented historical incidents

### Robustness Under Optimization

*Definition:* Does the measurement remain valid when systems are optimized to perform well on it?

*For Hardware Attestation:*
- Adversarial testing: Attempt to bypass attestation through optimization
- Formal analysis: Prove cryptographic properties hold under optimization
- Stress testing: Evaluate under extreme optimization pressure
