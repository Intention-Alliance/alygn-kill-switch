## Section 1: About Coefficient Giving

### Overview

Coefficient Giving (formerly Open Philanthropy's AI safety grantmaking) is a major funder of AI safety and security research. They take an evidence-based, transparent approach to grantmaking with significant resources ($40M+ available) and a willingness to support unconventional approaches.

The organization emerged from Open Philanthropy's AI safety portfolio and became independent to focus specifically on navigating transformative AI. They maintain strong ties to the Effective Altruism community while operating with a mission-focused, rather than organization-focused, approach.

### Leadership

Coefficient Giving's leadership brings deep expertise in AI safety and effective grantmaking:

- **Formerly part of Open Philanthropy** — inherits rigorous evaluation processes and evidence-based culture
- **Now independent** — mission-focused approach without organizational constraints
- **Strong technical advisory network** — connections across AI safety research community

### Culture & Values

Coefficient Giving operates with four core values that shape their grantmaking:

#### 1. Ownership

- Take responsibility holistically and personally
- Seek to understand reasoning behind work, not just execute tasks
- Do better version than what manager envisioned
- Ask questions to fully understand context
- Pride in work products according to own judgment
- Value time — seek efficient approaches

*ALYGN Alignment:* ALYGN's self-directed, mission-first approach matches this value perfectly. The team takes ownership of outcomes, not just tasks.

#### 2. Openness

- Give, seek, and receive feedback openly
- Critical feedback across all levels is encouraged
- Positive feedback equally essential
- Frequent checking in and course correction
- Open to errors in worldview
- Risk and mistake tolerance
- Make space for new ideas

*ALYGN Alignment:* ALYGN's transparency and feedback-seeking culture aligns with this value. The organization operates with open communication.

#### 3. Calibration

- Transparent reasoning about uncertainty
- Use forecasting practices (quantified probabilities)
- Quantitative and systematic frameworks
- Empirical and logical reasoning
- Don't defer to credentials alone

*ALYGN Alignment:* Evidence-based governance and decision-making fits Coefficient's calibration value. ALYGN values data-driven approaches.

#### 4. Inclusiveness

- Respect (free from harassment)
- Collegiality (building connections across teams)
- Balance outcomes with human collaboration

*ALYGN Alignment:* Multi-stakeholder coordination and collaborative values match this principle.

### Community & Approach

Coefficient Giving's grantmaking approach includes:

- **Rolling applications** — No fixed deadlines; submit when ready
- **Fast response times** — ~2 weeks for EOI, quick turnaround on proposals
- **Mission over organization loyalty** — Willing to fund the best approach regardless of organizational affiliation
- **Evidence-based decision making** — Transparent reasoning, quantified uncertainty
- **Willingness to fund new organizations** — Explicitly open to new entrants and unconventional approaches
- **Strong feedback culture** — Provide feedback even on declined applications

## Section 4: Full Proposal (If Invited)

If your EOI is accepted, you'll be invited to submit a full proposal. Here's what to prepare:

### Section 1: Project Summary

**Elevator Pitch (1 paragraph)**

> ALYGN is developing hardware-enforced AI safety compliance infrastructure that combines Trusted Execution Environments with cryptoeconomic incentive mechanisms. This creates verifiable, tamper-resistant safety enforcement that cannot be bypassed through software manipulation, directly addressing the control evaluations challenge in AI safety.

**Research Question**

> How can we create technical infrastructure for AI safety compliance that provides cryptographic guarantees of enforcement, remains robust to sophisticated circumvention attempts, and scales to advanced AI systems through automated verification?

**Alignment with Coefficient Priorities**

- **Primary:** Control Evaluations — hardware attestation enables verifiable control
- **Secondary:** Scalable Oversight — automated verification scales to superhuman systems
- **Tertiary:** AI Security — provides model containment mechanisms

### Section 2: Background & Motivation

**Current Limitations in AI Safety Controls**

Current AI safety mechanisms rely predominantly on software-based controls:
- Software can be modified, bypassed, or disabled
- Human oversight doesn't scale to superhuman systems
- Compliance is difficult to verify and audit
- No cryptographic guarantees of enforcement

**Why Hardware Enforcement is Necessary**

- **Tamper-resistance:** Hardware provides physical enforcement layer
- **Cryptographic guarantees:** TEEs provide mathematical proofs of execution
- **Scalability:** Automated verification without human bottlenecks
- **Auditability:** Immutable records of system states

**Threat Model**

What risks does this address:
- Sophisticated actors circumventing software controls
- Insider threats modifying safety systems
- Scalable oversight of superhuman capabilities
- Verifiable compliance for regulatory requirements
- Multi-stakeholder coordination with trust guarantees

### Section 3: Technical Approach

**TEE/SGX Architecture**

- Trusted Execution Environment overview
- Intel SGX / AMD SEV / ARM TrustZone comparison
- Attestation protocol design
- Integration with AI inference pipelines
- Performance considerations and optimizations

**Cryptoeconomic Mechanism Design**

- Bitcoin-based incentive structures
- Staking and slashing mechanisms
- Operator alignment through economic incentives
- Game-theoretic analysis of mechanism robustness
- Cost-benefit analysis for operators

**Blockchain Audit Trail Implementation**

- Bitcoin blockchain as immutable ledger
- Privacy-preserving compliance records
- Retrospective analysis capabilities
- Integration with existing monitoring systems
- Cost optimization for on-chain storage

**Validation Methodology**

- Security analysis and threat modeling
- Formal verification of critical components
- Empirical testing with real AI systems
- Benchmark development and validation
- Peer review and external audit

### Section 4: Research Area Alignment

**Control Evaluations Criteria**

Coefficient Giving defines Control Evaluations as:
- ✅ Tasks that mimic valuable work but could enable harm
- ✅ Blue team protocol for safe AI task completion
- ✅ Relies on AI/human oversight (not simple OWASP mitigations)

**Scalable Oversight Connection**

- Hardware attestation provides automated monitoring
- Cryptoeconomic incentives align without human enforcement
- Blockchain audit trails enable retrospective oversight
- Scales to systems beyond human comprehension
