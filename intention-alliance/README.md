# Intention Alliance - Project Overview

## Mission
Humanizing technology by empowering people online, facilitating monetizing their intentions while improving their attention.

## Core Contributions

### 1. Promotion of Liberty and Dignity Online
- Freedom of speech through decentralized journalism
- Freedom of attention (protecting against exploitation)
- Freedom of intentions (ethical monetization)

### 2. New Income Source
- For individuals
- For social media platforms

## Key Principles

### 1.1 Freedom of Speech
Decentralized journalism and communication foster freedom of speech. Intention Alliance fortifies this by monetizing decentralized journalism and communicators.

### 1.2 Freedom of Intentions
People's intentions online are being exploited without their consent. We provide a mechanism to utilize these ethically and financially lucratively.

### 1.3 Freedom of Attention
Like in "The Matrix," people need to protect their attention online. Having the ability to choose what you would like to see is crucial.

## Protocol Nature
**Intention Alliance is a PROTOCOL** — not just a technological solution.

> **Protocol meaning:** The official procedure or system of rules, governing models, affairs of state or diplomatic occasions.

The most critical element is **the alliance** that promotes empowering individuals by monetizing their intentions and optimizing their attention.
