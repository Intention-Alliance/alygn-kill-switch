## 2. What This License Covers (and What It Doesn't)

The GNU GPL v3 covers the **source code** of the Alygn Kill Switch. It does **not** grant any license to the Alygn Marks. The GPL v3 itself states (§7): "You may not impose any further restrictions" on the software — but trademark rights are independent of copyright, and this policy exercises those independent rights.

**In plain terms:** the code is free; the brand is protected.

## 3. Permitted Use

You MAY use the Alygn Marks to:

- **Refer to the software** — stating that your project uses, runs on, or is built with the Alygn Kill Switch ("nominative fair use"). Example: *"Our deployment pipeline integrates the Alygn Kill Switch for AI governance."*
- **Link to the project** — in documentation, blog posts, presentations, or academic work.
- **Contribute** — using the marks in the context of genuine contributions to this repository.
- **Describe compatibility** — stating that your product is compatible with the Alygn Kill Switch, provided the statement is true and not misleading.

## 4. Prohibited Use

You MAY NOT use the Alygn Marks to:

- **Name a derivative product** — a fork or modified version may not be called "Alygn Kill Switch," "ALYGN," or any confusingly similar name. Derivatives must be renamed (see §5 for white-label provisions).
- **Imply endorsement** — suggesting that Alygn, Intention Alliance, or Andler Devs Studio endorses, certifies, or sponsors your product, service, or organization.
- **Use the logo on derivative products** — the Alygn logo may not appear on white-labeled deployments, forked distributions, or modified versions presented as official.
- **Register confusingly similar marks** — domain names, company names, product names, or logos that create confusion with the Alygn brand.
- **Modify the logo** — altering proportions, colors, or composition of the Alygn logo in ways that compromise brand integrity.

## 5. White-Label Deployments

Organizations deploying the Kill Switch for their own operations may:

- Rename the dashboard and interface for their internal users (via the `NEXT_PUBLIC_BRAND_*` configuration variables).
- Operate the system under their own brand for their internal teams and customers.

Organizations deploying white-labeled instances **must**:

- Retain the attribution in the documentation ("Built on the ALYGN Kill Switch — https://github.com/Intention-Alliance/alygn-core-infra").
- Retain the sidebar footer credit ("Powered by ALYGN — Dignity Runtime") or an equivalent attribution approved by Intention Alliance.
- Not remove the LICENSE file or the copyright notices from the source code.

White-label deployments do **not** require a separate trademark license as long as the attribution requirements are met and the deployment is not marketed as an Alygn product.

The official Alygn logo is maintained in this repository at [`assets/alygn-logo.png`](assets/alygn-logo.png). Additional brand assets (SVG variants, favicon, cover images) are maintained by Intention Alliance and available upon request for legitimate uses described in §3.

- Do not recolor outside the approved brand palette (teal-cyan `#0891b2` on dark navy `#0a1728`).

## 7. Domain Names & Online Use

You may not register or use domain names, social media handles, or online identifiers that incorporate the Alygn Marks or any confusingly similar variation (including "alygn", "alygn-killswitch", "alygnks" and phonetic equivalents) without written permission. This does not restrict truthful references such as `github.com/Intention-Alliance/alygn-core-infra` or links to the official project.

If you become aware of use of the Alygn Marks that violates this policy, report it to **trademark@andler.dev**. Include the URL or context of the use, the specific mark involved, and any relevant details. Intention Alliance will review and take appropriate action.
